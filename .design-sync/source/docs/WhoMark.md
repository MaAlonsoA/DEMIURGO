Who did something: You, DEMIURGO, an Agent or Automatic.

Put it in the "who and when" zone of a card, beside each line of a story and on each message in a thread.

- `you`: the only one who confirms. On a check: verified by hand.
- `demiurgo`: drafts, asks and proposes. Pass the model as `name`; it shows on hover.
- `agent`: works from outside and only proposes. Pass its name.
- `automatic`: a rule, the import, a knowledge update. On a check: verified by a test.

```jsx
<WhoMark who="agent" name="Claude Code" label />
```
