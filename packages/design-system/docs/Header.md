The app header: the product name, the project, the sections and Needs you on the right.

56px tall on the surface color with a hairline bottom rule. The current tab is filled; Needs you, with its counter, always sits at the right.

In an app, `link` renders each tab, and Needs you, as the app's own link (for a router); Needs you is then always there, with its counter only when something waits. `actions` puts tools before Needs you: search, status, the person's menu.

```jsx
<Header project="DEMIURGO v2" tabs={['Product', 'Threads', 'Knowledge', 'History']} current="Product" needs={2} />
```
