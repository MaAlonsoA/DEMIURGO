# What H1 builds

H1 is the first milestone with an interface. It is done when the FDR of S3 is born and approved inside DEMIURGO, with the person in the UI. The spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-24-interfaz-h1-design.md` in the repository, with ADR-WEB-001 (the stack) and FDR-INT-001 (the behavior and its checks).

## The path

1. **Take over the design:** sign in, open the imported package, check its counts against `design/` and ratify it in one step.
2. **See your product:** DEMIURGO's own decisions, tech decisions and features, with how sure, ready or not, and what needs you.
3. **Approve what you agree with:** a record, its versions and approving one.
4. **Explore S3:** a thread with DEMIURGO until the questions settle.
5. **Ask for a draft:** DEMIURGO runs, and a package with the feature and its checks arrives.
6. **Change it:** a new version with a note, carrying each check.
7. **Ready to build.**

Along the way H1 also needs:
- Needs you with every kind of item;
- errors in plain words;
- runs;
- knowledge;
- sources.

An agent through the terminal channel reuses the proposal pattern and has no screens of its own.

## What each screen gives H1

| Label | Screens |
|---|---|
| **H1, as they are** | the blueprint, the language (dots, color, icons, who, card, point-peek-keep), the legend, one part at a time, ready to build, an agent's proposals |
| **H1, adapted** | Overview and Origins (records instead of features, people and rules), the stage bars (only the first lives), the first question (a question in a thread), the feature at a glance (Behavior as text, no lanes), changing something (a new version carrying each check), welcome back, catching up |
| **Later** | Map (S7), Journeys, the day-1 reading of a whole idea (S6), you're up to date (S6) |
| **Not for now** | connecting an agent, the agent's page |

Not in H1: *Models & providers*, which needs its own FDR first.
