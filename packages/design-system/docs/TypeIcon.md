What something is: one stroke icon per type, always first on a card.

Stroke icons on a 24 grid, round caps, drawn in currentColor so they follow the label color. 14px on cards, 20px in references. With `label`, the type word follows in the uppercase label style.

Types: `feature`, `decision`, `tech-decision`, `question`, `check`, `idea`, `thread`, `journey`, `bug`. The same strokes, and the signal icons, are available as `Icon`. The Build stage has no icon: it is the StageBars track.

```jsx
<TypeIcon type="feature" label />
```
